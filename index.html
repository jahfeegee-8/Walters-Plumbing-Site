COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

Have a question before booking? Here are straight answers to the questions homeowners most frequently ask us.

How quickly can you attend to a plumbing emergency in Kempton Park? +

For urgent emergencies like burst geysers, active ceiling leaks, or overflowing drains in Kempton Park and immediate suburbs, we aim to be on-site within 30 to 60 minutes. Please call us directly on 084 777 4975 for fastest dispatch rather than sending an email.

Do you provide upfront pricing and free quotes? +

Yes! We believe in transparent, honest pricing with zero hidden fees. For standard jobs, renovations, tiling, and installations, we assess the scope of work and provide a clear quote before any work starts so you know exactly what to expect.

What should I do if my geyser bursts or stops heating? +

First, immediately switch off the geyser circuit breaker on your main electrical distribution board, and turn off your main municipal water shutoff valve. Then call Walter right away. We can replace faulty thermostats, elements, or vacuum valves, or install a brand-new compliant SABS geyser complete with drip tray, pressure valve, and insurance-ready invoice.

Can you clear stubborn blocked drains without digging up my yard? +

Yes. In the vast majority of cases, blocked drains (toilets, kitchen lines, sewer pipes, and stormwater gullies) can be cleared using our specialized mechanical drain rods and clearing machines. We only recommend digging as a last resort if a pipe has suffered structural collapse or severe root rupture.

Do you do tiling and full bathroom renovations as well as plumbing? +

Yes! Unlike contractors who only fit pipes and leave unfinished holes in walls, Walter Plumbers handles the complete installation from rough-in plumbing to sanitaryware fitting and precision wall and floor tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, and showers.

Are your parts and workmanship guaranteed? +

Yes. All workmanship carried out by Walter Plumbers is 100% guaranteed. We only use SABS-approved piping, fittings, and valves from trusted plumbing manufacturers to ensure reliable, long-lasting durability for your home.
